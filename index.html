Healthcare SaaS Live

FastCare

Connecting patients, hospitals, and India's government healthcare schemes on one intelligent digital platform.

FastCare digitizes the three biggest pain points in Indian public healthcare: scheme discovery, hospital onboarding, and claim submission with AI fraud detection. Eligible citizens across India get auto-matched to schemes like Ayushman Bharat (₹5L/family), CGHS, ESIC, and PMJDY — all without paperwork.

  • 🤖
    AI Anomaly Detection — 7-factor fraud scoring engine flags suspicious claims in real-time before processing
  • 📋
    Scheme Eligibility Matching — Auto-matches patients to AB-PMJAY, CGHS, ESIC & PMJDY based on Aadhaar & profile
  • 🏥
    Hospital Onboarding Wizard — 6-step guided setup for clinics to list doctors, treatments & supported schemes
  • 📁
    Digital Health Records — Portable, Aadhaar-linked patient record wallet across all visits and hospitals
